Generated by LLM/record/design.py, seed 7589887162087371572: cubic 1 -> 250 Hz at mean 9.7 Hz/s, hold 10 s.

| segment | from Hz | to Hz | seconds | mode | k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 250 | 25.67 | POLY | 3 |

## Randomised coil duty

Mode **design**. per-channel multiplier on the ramp's carrier duty (collective*duty/100); duty=100 on all coils sent in IDLE before seq=go; this duty sent by the host when the PLANNED frequency reaches 230 Hz (capture meta host_duty_switch_send_complete_s); reset clears it.
Host sent: `duty=99.2:86.8:96.1:98.5`.

| channel | planned % | device-applied % (median while driven) | powered (peak A) | effective % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 99.2 | 100.0 | yes (2.25) | 99.2 |
| B | 86.8 | 100.0 | yes (2.52) | 86.8 |
| C | 96.1 | 100.0 | yes (2.52) | 96.1 |
| D | 98.5 | 100.0 | yes (2.02) | 98.5 |

Applied = the ramp's carrier duty times planned/100, as the device reports it; the current that flowed is `current.csv` `current_*_A`. Duty is not current.
