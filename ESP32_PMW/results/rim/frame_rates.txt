Frame rates, rim J(f) experiment (whole ring), 640x400
===================================================

`measured` is the capture rate actually achieved: the number to quote, and the one
that sets the practical limit for resolving the spin line (measured/2 Hz).
`declared` is what the mp4 plays at; `dropped` is frames the encoder queue lost.
Only rows marked `use` belong in the analysis; `superseded` takes are kept on disk
from interrupted runs or the pre-2026-09-24 single-segment ramp.

Rates are recorded PER TAKE rather than fixed across the series, because a higher
request did not deliver a higher rate: the pipeline is encoder-bound, not
sensor-bound. The 2026-09-24 blocks were filmed with no rate requested, so the
sensor kept its own default; from `cap_fps` onward the driver is asked for its
clamp. See the notes below.

notes
-----
* `measured` is the rate to quote. It fell to ~194 fps with the encoder running
  against ~209 fps for the bare source, so the pipeline is encoder-bound, not
  sensor-bound: asking for more does not deliver more.
* 271.3 fps is the SENSOR-only figure from `modes.py`. Over `open_source` the
  AVFoundation driver clamps a higher request to 210.0 (measured 2026-09-27), and
  `record.CAP_FPS_CEILING` now asks for 210 so `granted` reads back honestly.
* `declared` above `measured` means the mp4 plays slightly fast. The analysis is
  unaffected: every time is taken from `frames.csv`, which stamps real capture
  times, not from frame indices divided by the declared rate.
