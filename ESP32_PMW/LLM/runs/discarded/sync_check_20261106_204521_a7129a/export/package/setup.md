## Frequency ramp

Generated by LLM/record/design.py, seed 2328255072397917483: cubic 1 -> 230 Hz at mean 9.7 Hz/s, hold 10 s.

| segment | from Hz | to Hz | seconds | mode | k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 230 | 23.608 | POLY | 3 |
| 2 | 230 | 230 | 10 | POLY | 1 |

Sent to the ESP32: `seq=clear`, `seq=ramp:1:230:23608:0:3`, `seq=ramp:230:230:10000:0:1`, `seq=go`
Planned f(t): `command_planned.csv`; device-reported frequency: `current.csv` (`frequency_reported_hz` vs `t_s`).

## Randomised coil duty

Mode **design**. per-channel multiplier on the ramp's carrier duty (collective*duty/100); duty=100 on all coils sent in IDLE before seq=go; this duty sent by the host when the PLANNED frequency reaches 200 Hz (capture meta host_duty_switch_send_complete_s); reset clears it.
Host sent: `duty=100.0:100.0:100.0:100.0`.

| channel | planned % | device-applied % (median while driven) | powered (peak A) | effective % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 100 | 100.0 | yes (2.30) | 100 |
| B | 100 | 100.0 | yes (2.37) | 100 |
| C | 100 | 100.0 | yes (2.06) | 100 |
| D | 100 | 100.0 | yes (1.88) | 100 |

Applied = the ramp's carrier duty times planned/100, as the device reports it; the current that flowed is `current.csv` `current_*_A`. Duty is not current.
